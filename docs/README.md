# ForexPulse Pro Terminal

Welcome to the **ForexPulse Pro Terminal**, a high-performance, web-based trading dashboard designed for professional analysis, risk management, and decision support.

## Overview

ForexPulse is not just a trading platform; it is a **Decision HQ**. Whether you execute trades directly within the app or use it as a companion for live trading on other platforms, ForexPulse provides the mathematical and analytical edge needed to succeed in the Forex markets.

### Key Value Propositions

1. **Price Integrity**: Use our live data feed as a "Source of Truth" to verify your broker's prices and spreads.
2. **Mathematical Edge**: Leverage the Precision Risk Calculator to ensure every trade follows strict money management rules.
3. **Market Intelligence**: Track global market sessions and high-impact economic events in real-time.
4. **Strategic suggestions**: Use our multi-indicator signal generator to confirm your directional bias.

## Quick Start

1. **Setup**: Run `npm run dev` to launch the terminal locally.
2. **Watchlist**: Add your favorite pairs to the Watchlist to start receiving live price updates and trade signals.
3. **Timeframe**: Switch between 15M, 1H, 4H, and 1D timeframes to align with your trading strategy.
4. **Customize**: Use the Settings page to define your default leverage, risk percentage, and UI theme.
